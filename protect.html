    Export

    Two separate downloads, on purpose

    1. The protected documents

    Safe to upload

    Names and other detected details are replaced with tokens. This is the archive you give to the AI service. It also contains a suggested prompt that keeps the tokens intact and makes the feedback easy to split back apart.

    2. Your private re-identification map

    Never upload this

    This file says which token means which student. Keep it as carefully as your gradebook, and delete it once you have restored the feedback. You need it again on the Restore page.

    There is no recovery. If you forget this, the map cannot be opened and you would have to match feedback to students by hand.
